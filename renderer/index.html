            Profile & zones

            Standard 5-zone model: 50–60, 60–70, 70–80, 80–90 and 90–100% of max heart rate. Add a resting HR to use heart rate reserve (Karvonen) instead.

            Works with Garmin, Polar, Wahoo, Coros, Suunto, Whoop, Scosche, Coospo and any sensor using the standard Heart Rate profile. On a watch, turn on heart rate broadcast first.
